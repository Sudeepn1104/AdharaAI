1. The Tenant shall not sublet the premises without obtaining prior written consent from the Landlord on each and every occasion.

2. The Landlord reserves the right to increase the rent at his sole discretion without prior notice.

3. No pets of any kind shall be permitted on the premises. Violation shall result in immediate eviction without notice or refund of deposit.

4. The Tenant shall give 30 days written notice before requesting any maintenance or repair work from the Landlord.

5. The Tenant agrees to all future amendments to this agreement as deemed fit by the Landlord without requiring further consent.

6. No guests shall be permitted to stay overnight without prior written permission from the Landlord on each occasion.

7. The Tenant shall not approach any consumer court, civil court, or legal forum for any dispute arising from this agreement.

8. The Tenant shall pay all electricity, water, maintenance charges, property tax, and any other charges levied by any authority.

9. The Tenant shall vacate the premises within 24 hours of receiving notice from the Landlord.

10. The agreement shall be binding on the Tenant's legal heirs and successors without any further consent.
