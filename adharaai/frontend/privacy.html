Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Version 1.0
⚠ Not legal advice. AdharaAI is a student research project and an AI tool. It does not provide legal advice, legal opinions, or legal representation. Always consult a qualified lawyer before making decisions based on any legal document.

1. What we collect

When you upload a document, we temporarily store:

  • The text content of your uploaded file (for analysis only)
  • The filename and file size
  • The analysis results (clause-level risk flags and simplified text)

We do not collect your name, email, IP address (beyond server logs), or any account information.

2. How long we keep your data

🔒 Your raw document text is automatically and permanently deleted within 5 minutes of upload — immediately after analysis is complete, or after 5 minutes if analysis is not triggered.

After deletion, we retain only the processed output (simplified clauses and risk flags) linked to an anonymous document ID. This output contains no personally identifying information from your document.

3. Who sees your data

  • Your document is processed entirely on our server — it is not sent to any third-party AI service or API
  • No human reads your document
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party

4. Cookies and tracking

AdharaAI does not use cookies, analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or any third-party tracking scripts.

5. Your rights

You can request deletion of any analysis results by using the DELETE /api/documents/{id} endpoint or by contacting us. Since we do not collect identifying information, we cannot link results to you unless you provide the document ID.

6. Security

  • All connections use HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • File uploads are validated for type and size before processing
  • Rate limiting prevents automated abuse
  • Server logs do not contain document content

7. Children

AdharaAI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.

9. Contact

This is a student research project from the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning. For questions, open an issue on our GitHub repository.
